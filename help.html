Frequently asked

Open Payment & Balances and click Record a payment. Enter the full amount the tenant handed over, then set Covers to the months it settles — for example "Aug balance + Sep". The system applies the oldest balance first and carries the remainder forward.
Record exactly what was received. The unpaid remainder stays on the tenant as an outstanding balance and appears in the Outstanding tab and on the dashboard, with the day count ticking from the original due date.
Open the tenant in Renter Information and choose Move out. The unit flips to Vacant, future rent charges are cancelled, and the tenant's portal login is turned off. To take an empty unit off the market, edit the unit and set it to Under repair.
Yes. The dashboard has Send reminder to all tenants under Rent collection, and the Outstanding tab has Remind everyone. Each tenant with a balance gets a notice in their tenant portal showing what they owe.
Tenants pick a charge, send the money by GCash, Maya, bank, or cash, and upload a screenshot with the reference number. It appears as Pending in Payment & Balances. Open it, check the reference, and click Confirm payment — the charge is marked paid and the tenant is notified. Reject tells them it could not be matched.
Only administrators can sign in to this console, and they see everything. Tenants sign in to the tenant portal and see only their own unit, charges, payments, and requests. Caretakers and crew are contacts you assign repairs to. Manage them in Settings → Staff & crew.
Go to Settings → Data & backup and choose Export — it downloads one JSON file with units, tenants, charges, payments, and repairs. For a full restorable copy, use phpMyAdmin → saraza_apartment → Export.
